Jiko colour board · v2

A full review of the three v1 palettes against the brand brief, with every problem measured and fixed. One direction was replaced outright. All figures on this page are calculated live from the hex values.

Recommendation: Onsen Dawn v2

  1. It's the only direction that hits every brief point. It's warm and supportive, clearly the snow monkey's world (snow ground, onsen teal, persimmon lamps), gender-neutral, and nothing like Finch.
  2. Jiko is now the star. The face moved from coral to rose, so it no longer blends with the persimmon buttons, and Jiko's silhouette clears 2.5:1 against every wall.
  3. The Spark Gem is its own thing: lantern gold with an onsen-teal glint. It no longer looks like a button.
  4. Night mode is ownable. Generic navy became onsen night, a deep teal-black, so dark mode feels like the hut after dark.
  5. It's a complete system. Five stat colours, status colours and a fear-forecast scale that never uses alarm red.

Method: text contrast uses WCAG 2.1 (AA = 4.5:1 for body text). "Distinct" uses CIELAB ΔE76, where 25+ reads as clearly different hues at a glance. Jiko's silhouette is the fur's luminance contrast against the wall and the rug Jiko sits on; Jiko also gets a darker fur edge. The Jiko drawing is a placeholder sketch, and Nunito is a placeholder font.
