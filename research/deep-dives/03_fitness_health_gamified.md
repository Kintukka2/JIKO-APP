# 03: Gamified physical health, fitness, nutrition, sleep and money-on-the-line apps

Research date: 2026-11-22. Scope: apps that turn healthier behaviour (moving, eating, drinking water, sleeping, quitting substances) into a game, a collection, a competition or a wager.

## How to read this

- **Store data was pulled live on 2026-11-22.** iOS figures come from the iTunes Search API (US storefront: average rating / rating count / first release date). Google Play figures come from `google_play_scraper` (US: score / rating count / install band / the scraper's "realInstalls" estimate). These are the numbers marked "iOS" and "GP" below.
- **Revenue, download, funding and efficacy figures are cited.** Third-party estimates from Sensor Tower, AppMagic, Sacra and similar are labelled as estimates. Anything I did not verify in this session is marked *(unverified, recollection)*.
- "n/f" means not found in this session.
- Sources are listed at the end of each section and again at the bottom of the file.

## A. Story and adventure games built on running or walking

### 1. Zombies, Run! (ZRX: Zombies Run + Marvel Move)
- **Developer:** Six to Start (London). OliveX acquired the studio in 2021 for an initial $6.65M plus up to $2.85M earn-out. Co-creator and writer Naomi Alderman reacquired Zombies, Run! from OliveX in Nov 2025 (PocketGamer.biz). The studio had redundancies in Oct 2023, and CEO Adrian Hon left.
- **Platforms / launch:** iOS (Feb 2012), Android. Apple Watch support. Companion apps: Zombies, Run! 5k Training (2012) and Marvel Move.
- **Core loop:** An audio drama plays in your headphones while you run in the real world. More than 500 story missions run across seasons with a written cast. Random "zombie chases" make you speed up by a set amount (interval training disguised as story). Supplies you pick up mid-run feed a light base-building meta-game (Abel Township). Progression comes from seasons and episodes. There are no leaderboards in the core experience. It is a narrative-first game.
- **Audience:** Recreational runners and walkers, many of them beginners who dislike running. The fan base skews toward women and book, podcast and genre fans. Strong accessibility reputation (walking is allowed).
- **Monetization:** It started as a paid app ($7.99) after a $72,627 Kickstarter from 3,464 backers. It switched to free-to-play plus subscription in late 2014. Current US IAP list: Membership $6.99 / $49.99, Legacy $24.99 to $29.99, VIP $89.99/yr. An older AU listing showed $5.99/mo or $39.99/yr.
- **Store:** iOS 4.79 (24,155 ratings). 5k Training iOS 4.81 (3,003). GP 3.9 (27,096 ratings), 1M+ band, about 3.3M scraped installs.
- **Scale:** About 10M downloads, "hundreds of thousands of active players and tens of thousands of subscribers" (company blog, 2022). An undated older report gave 8.5M downloads, 300k MAU and 50k paying.
- **Efficacy:** No RCT found. Plenty of user testimony about couch-to-5k success.
- **Users love:** The writing and voice acting, a reason to go out the door ("I need to know what happens next"), and that it works for walkers.
- **Complaints:** Android rating is much lower (GPS and audio bugs). Subscription wall over old content. Pace of new content slowed after the layoffs.
- **Status and lessons:** Alive for 14 years, which makes it the longest-lived title in this category. **Why it endured:** (1) The fiction delivers intrinsic motivation tied to the activity itself, without points. (2) It sits on top of a behaviour people already do, so it is a game you take on your run rather than another app to open. (3) A large back-catalogue makes a subscription worth keeping. (4) It was cheap to run (audio only, no 3D). **Weakness:** Content is expensive to produce. When the studio was cut back, content slowed and growth stalled. A narrative moat needs steady writing output.
- Sources: Wikipedia; PocketGamer.biz (Kickstarter, redundancies, Alderman reacquisition); blog.zombiesrungame.com "Ten years"; App Store listings.

### 2. Pokémon GO
- **Developer:** Niantic, with The Pokémon Company and Nintendo. The app is now published by Scopely, which took over Niantic's games business in 2025 (store seller is "Scopely Explore, Inc."). Deal widely reported at about $3.5B *(figure unverified in this session)*.
- **Platforms / launch:** iOS and Android, July 2016.
- **Core loop:** AR location game. Walk to find, catch and collect Pokémon. Spin PokéStops. Battle in gyms and raids. Hatch eggs by walking 2 to 12 km. Buddy distance. Adventure Sync counts steps in the background. Weekly fitness rewards. Live events such as Community Day and GO Fest.
- **Monetization:** Free-to-play IAP (raid passes, incubators, storage), $0.99 to $99.99. Paid event tickets.
- **Store:** iOS 4.00 (657k ratings). GP 3.46 (15.4M ratings), 500M+ band, about 568M scraped installs.
- **Revenue:** More than $9B lifetime, AppMagic/Sensor Tower IAP estimates. Peak year was Jul 2020 to Jul 2021 at about $1.4B. Scopely reportedly said 2025 total revenue topped $1B. GO Fest 2026 drove a four-year-high month of $141.5M (PocketGamer.biz).
- **Efficacy (the key evidence in this category):**
  - Howe et al. (BMJ, 2016) found **+955 steps/day in week 1**, with the effect **gone after 6 weeks**.
  - Althoff, White & Horvitz (JMIR, 2016) studied 32k Microsoft Band users and found a significant activity increase, extrapolated to about 144B extra US steps.
  - The lesson from both: novelty-driven activity gains decay unless the game keeps refreshing.
- **Users love:** Exploration, collecting, social raids with strangers, events that bring people to parks.
- **Complaints:** Monetization creep, remote raid pass price hikes (2023 backlash), the 2023 avatar redesign, cheating and spoofing, and the rural vs urban gap. Android rating 3.46.
- **Lessons:** Collection plus place plus social events can mobilise very large numbers of people. The health effect is a by-product and fades for most players. The players who stay are the ones attached to events and community.

### 3. Pokémon Sleep
- **Developer:** The Pokémon Company and SELECT BUTTON. Launched July 2023, iOS and Android.
- **Core loop:** Put the phone on the bed (or use the Pokémon GO Plus+ accessory). The app tracks sleep stages and gives a "sleep score". Pokémon gather around Snorlax based on your sleep type (dozing, snoozing, slumbering). Each morning you "research" which Pokémon showed up. A daytime helper and cooking meta-game power Snorlax up. Weekly cycle. Collecting a "sleep style dex".
- **Monetization:** Free-to-play IAP ($1.19 to $97.99) plus Premium Pass (about $9.99/mo *(unverified)*).
- **Store:** iOS 4.54 (25,414). GP 4.4 (139,755), 10M+ band, about 13.6M scraped installs.
- **Revenue:** Reported at about $182.8M in two years and $234.9M by the third anniversary (PocketGamer.biz/Outlook Respawn, AppMagic-style estimates). In its first year Japan was 37% of downloads and **70% of revenue** (Sensor Tower). US iOS Q2 2025: weekly revenue $42k to $126k, active users falling from 159k to 128k.
- **Efficacy:** University of Tsukuba (Sleep Health, 2025) studied 2,063 users of Pokémon Sleep plus the ASKEN diet app. Group-level changes were modest, **45.3% increased total sleep time**, and sleep gains correlated with BMI reduction. One author had consulted for The Pokémon Company. Company data from Japanese 3-month retained users showed about +30 min after 1 month and about +70 min after 3 months (self-selected).
- **Users love:** It makes "going to bed" a reward. Cute morning reveal. Low effort.
- **Complaints:** Phone-on-mattress tracking, a battery drain, repetitive gameplay. Heavy grind for the cooking/Snorlax layer.
- **Lessons:** The morning "reveal" is a strong variable reward for a behaviour that is otherwise invisible. Beyond Japan the market is much smaller. Revenue is event-driven.

### 4. Pikmin Bloom
- **Developer:** Niantic with Nintendo, now Scopely. Launched Oct 2021.
- **Core loop:** Walk to grow and collect Pikmin, plant flowers along your path (a visible trail on the map), collect "decor" Pikmin by location type, run expeditions, Big Flowers and community-day events. Gentle and non-competitive. Weekly challenges with friends. Lifelogging of "memories".
- **Monetization:** Free-to-play IAP ($0.99 to $219.99), Community Day tickets.
- **Store:** **iOS 4.81 (71,395)**, GP 4.76 (260,511), 5M+ band, about 9.4M scraped installs. These are the highest ratings in the location-game group.
- **Revenue:** Started slowly ($5.3M in its first 6 months, Sensor Tower). It crossed $100M lifetime on 1 Dec 2025, and 2025 was its best year. Year 5 brought $36.1M. A July 2026 report put lifetime revenue at $139.4M, with Taiwan spending up 902%. Sources conflict on the lifetime total (PocketGamer.biz via AppMagic).
- **Lessons:** A slow-burn cozy walking game can **grow revenue every year**, unlike Pokémon GO's decline. The keys are a calm, low-pressure loop, collection and visible traces of your walks. It strongly supports a "gentle" gamification thesis.

### 5. Walkr: Fitness Space Adventure
- **Developer:** Sparkful (formerly Fourdesire, Taiwan). Launched in 2014.
- **Core loop:** Steps produce energy. You fly a spaceship to discover and build up planets. Collect 50+ planets and satellites, DSA missions, a co-op "Labs" with friends.
- **Monetization:** Free with IAP and gems ($0.99 to $99.99).
- **Store:** iOS 4.53 (8,087). GP 4.19 (77,043), 1M+ band, about 1.2M installs.
- **Lessons:** An early "steps as currency" collection game. Engagement is high at first, and the game gets grindy and timer-heavy later. Sparkful reused the pattern in Plant Nanny.

### 6. Fantasy Hike
- **Developer:** Forge7 AB (Sweden). Launched in 2021.
- **Core loop:** Real steps move your character along a fantasy trail. Distance-based "virtual hike" collectibles (comparable to Conqueror Challenges).
- **Store:** iOS **4.83 (15,112)**. Not found on GP.
- **Note:** A small team with high ratings, which shows that step-to-journey fiction is well liked.

### 7. Walking RPG: Fitness Hero Game (and Sleep Hero)
- **Developer:** Shikudo Co., Ltd. Launched in 2018.
- **Loop:** An idle-RPG battler powered by step count. You gain gear, fight bosses and level up as you walk. In 2025 the developer added "Sleep Hero: Bedtime Habit Game".
- **Store:** iOS 4.65 (2,131). GP 4.29 (8,950), 1M+ band, about 1.5M installs.

### 8. Run An Empire
- **Developer:** Location Games Ltd (London). Funded on Kickstarter, iOS launch about 2016–2018.
- **Loop:** Real-world territory conquest. Run loops to claim hexes, defend them against others, and advance your civilization from the stone age to the space age. Castles and battles. A global competition mode was added in v3.12.
- **Store:** iOS 4.50 (3,544). GP 3.92, 100k+ band.
- **Complaints:** Progress freezes, GPS spikes that void runs, a thin tutorial, slow updates. Press called it "Risk for runners" (Fast Company, Runner's World).
- **Status:** Still listed, but no 2025–26 activity found. **Lesson:** The territory idea was ahead of its time and then stalled, because small teams cannot support real-time multiplayer, anti-cheat and GPS edge cases.

### 9. INTVL (and the 2024–26 territory-running wave: KYRO, Captivate, Terrarun, Runquest)
- **Developer:** INTVL Pty Ltd (Melbourne). Founded 2024. Co-founders Louis Phillips and Jordan Hesse.
- **Loop:** **Paper.io-style territory running.** The loop you run becomes your territory. Others can "take over" your ground live. Clubs pool territory. Leaderboards. Bike Mode added in July 2026.
- **Spread:** Broke out on TikTok in late 2024 (its "Terra" feature).
- **Store:** iOS 4.73 (5,778). GP 4.3 (13,279), 1M+ band, about 1.5M installs. IAP $7.99 to $79.99.
- **Scale:** 2.5M+ downloads in 182 countries and 3.5M territories captured. The US is 46% of users and Australia 13.5% (company press release, July 2026).
- **Copycats:**
  - **KYRO** (Movements Lab, Korea, Jan 2026): iOS 4.0 (35), GP 2.58, 100k+.
  - Captivate, Terrarun, Runquest and capture.territory: all tiny.
- **Complaints:** GPS dropouts end territory claims. Griefing and "stolen" turf frustrates people.
- **Lesson:** Territory is **the hottest new running mechanic of 2024–26**. It works because it is social, visible on a map and creates rivalry. It makes the city into a game board, the same way Pokémon GO did.

### 10. Ring Fit Adventure / Fitness Boxing (console reference)
- **Ring Fit Adventure:** Nintendo, Switch, Oct 2019. A turn-based RPG where attacks are physical exercises done with the Ring-Con and a leg strap. Overworld jogging, a boss story, skill trees. It sold **14.09M by March 2022** (Nintendo). Strong COVID-era sales and shortages.
- **Fitness Boxing (1 and 2):** Imagineer, published by Nintendo outside Japan. A rhythm-boxing game. The first game passed 1M (Sept 2020) and the series reached 2.5M.
- **Lessons:** A packaged, one-off purchase with an RPG wrapper can sell tens of millions. Exercises are reframed as "attacks", which is the clearest example of mechanics mapped onto movement. Many players stopped partway (the game takes 80+ hours). Players say the game makes them want to keep exercising, but motivation drops in the late game.

### 11. Fitness RPGs and "level up" apps (Solo Leveling wave, 2024–26)
- **Arise: Workout & Fitness RPG** (Golden Gate Media / "Arise: Level Up Workout Game", Digital Lions Apps, Apr 2025): **GP 4.56 (275,952 ratings), 1M+ band, about 2.2M installs. iOS 4.76 (20,989).** Clearly inspired by the anime *Solo Leveling*. A "System" assigns daily quests (push-ups, runs). You earn XP, stats and E-to-S rank. Hard paywall (Arise Pro, $4.99 to $59.99).
- **Others in the wave:** Monarch – Level Up Workouts (E-to-SSS ranks, guilds, subscription), Level Up – Gamified Fitness ($9.99/mo, $49.99/yr), ARISE SOLO (GP 4.58, 50k+), Ascended: Fitness RPG (Level Up Fitness Inc., Jun 2026, iOS 4.63, 89 ratings), Level-UP: Fitness (iOS 3.98, 177), Fitscape (iOS 4.09, 75), Workout Quest (GP 3.38), RPGFitness, Fitness RPG: Tower of Aggani.
- **"Fit Quest" / "FitQuest"** search: only tiny 2026 indie apps (0 to 1 ratings). No notable product uses this name.
- **"Habit Fitness RPG":** No single product. Life Reset: 66 Day Habit (Savvy Tech, 2024, iOS 4.71, 13,319) and Kaizen AI are the closest. Habitica (iOS 4.33/3,731, GP 4.63/76,522, 5M+, about 8.4M installs) is the general-purpose reference, and many users set up fitness dailies in it.
- **Lesson:** **Anime "System" or "rank-up" framing is a proven 2025 acquisition hook** (Arise reached about 276k GP ratings within roughly 18 months). Retention evidence is n/f. Reviews of the category mention paywalls and generic workouts under a thin RPG skin.

## B. Earn, wager or donate per step (money on the line)

### 12. StepBet (WayBetter)
- **Developer:** WayBetter Inc. (same family as DietBet). The App Store copyright now reads FitnessAI Inc., which suggests FitnessAI acquired or merged with WayBetter *(details n/f)*.
- **Launch / platforms:** iOS Jan 2016, Android (`com.stepbet.app`).
- **Loop:** Pay to join a 6-week (or shorter) game, typically $40. Hit personalised "active" and "stretch" step targets 4 to 6 days a week based on your baseline. Winners split the pot after the house takes about 15%. Social feed with an "Referee".
- **Store:** iOS **4.79 (23,634)**. GP 4.5 (9,601), 1M+ band, about 1.8M installs.
- **Win rates:** No official figure. Reviews cite about 60–70% winners per game. Money Crashers models a 700/1,000 win case giving about $8.57 profit each. WayBetter's own claim is that money-on-the-line players are "5x more likely to succeed" (marketing).
- **Complaints:** Sync failures cost people the game, fees eat winnings, "won but lost money after fees" (Trustpilot), strict rules.
- **Lesson:** Loss aversion works, and a self-calibrating target keeps it fair. Earnings are small, so the real draw is the commitment.

### 13. DietBet (WayBetter)
- **Launch:** iOS Jun 2012. GP `com.dietbet.app` (4.69, 14,243 ratings, 500k+).
- **Loop:** "Kickstarter" game: lose 4% in 4 weeks. "Transformer": 10% in 6 months. Photo weigh-ins checked by referees. Pot split among winners.
- **Store:** iOS 4.77 (20,575).
- **Win rates:** Anecdotally 62–70% (one game 264/384 = 69%). Average payouts of about $50–60.
- **Complaints:** Weigh-in hacks (crash dieting or dehydration before the final weigh-in), small profits, rejected weigh-ins.

### 14. HealthyWage
- **Developer:** HealthyWage LLC (NYC). Founded about 2009. iOS Jul 2015, GP `com.healthywage` (4.5, 6,558 ratings, 100k+).
- **Loop:** A personal bet. You choose the goal, timeline and monthly stake, and a calculator shows a payout of up to about $10k. Also team challenges and employer programs (this is where most of the revenue comes from *(unverified)*). Video weigh-ins.
- **Store:** iOS 4.79 (17,870).
- **Win rate:** Founder told NPR that "about 30 to 40% of participants win" (self-reported).
- **Efficacy basis:**
  - Volpp et al. (JAMA 2008), a deposit-contract and lottery trial: incentive groups lost more weight, but much of it came back after the incentives stopped.
  - A 2016 JAMA Internal Medicine trial: 39% of the incentive group lost at least 5% of body weight at 6 months, and the gap narrowed by 12 months.
  - Cornell / Journal of Health Economics: forfeitable bonds produced the most weight loss.
- **Complaints:** Strict verification and disputes. Bets you lose are a large source of money for the company. Critics in The Outline called it "a game you can't win". Encourages crash dieting near deadlines.
- **Lesson:** Deposit contracts work in the short term and regain is common. **Ethics:** the business profits when users fail, which is a reputational risk.

### 15. Sweatcoin
- **Developer:** Sweatco Ltd (London). iOS Sep 2016, Android 2017.
- **Loop:** Verified outdoor steps are converted to "sweatcoins" (about 1 per 1,000 steps after a 5% "commission"). Coins are spent in a marketplace of brand offers and charity donations. Friends referral. Tiered "membership" levels gate how much you can earn each month. In Sep 2022 it launched the **SWEAT crypto token** (on NEAR) through a separate Sweat Wallet.
- **Monetization:** Advertising and affiliate offers (brands pay for exposure and leads), Premium subscriptions, IAP ($0.99 to $36.99), and token-related income.
- **Store:** iOS 4.53 (404,053). GP 4.48 (3.2M ratings), 100M+ band, about 164M installs. Sweat Wallet iOS 4.74 (34,350).
- **Revenue and downloads:** About $22M (2022) and $20M (2023). 23.9M downloads in 2023, down 60% YoY (Business of Apps). The company claims 120M+ users and 50B+ SWEAT minted (May 2024).
- **Crypto collapse:** SWEAT hit an all-time low of $0.000175 (May 2026). Market cap was about $3–5M in July 2026. Emissions were cut 37% in March 2024. **The Sweat Foundation announced that Sweat Wallet and the SWEAT token "as they exist today end on 30 Dec 2026".** Sweatco says the Sweatcoin app will continue as normal.
- **Efficacy:** Elliott et al. (BJSM 2019, Sweatco-affiliated) studied about 6,000 users and found +19.5% daily steps over 6 months vs the 3 months before. A JMIR mHealth 2019 study of 5,892 users found +18.7%. BMC Public Health 2021 found short-term gains that were **not sustained at 12 months**.
- **Complaints:** Coins are nearly worthless and the marketplace is full of ads. Steps are not counted indoors or on treadmills. Earning limits. Crypto disappointment.
- **Lesson:** Micro-rewards drive downloads (164M+ Android) but small payouts lose their pull and the token economy failed. The ad and affiliate model outlasted the crypto layer.

### 16. STEPN (and STEPN GO) — move-to-earn case study
- **Developer:** FindSatoshi Lab (Australia / China team). Beta late 2021, app Feb 2022 (iOS). STEPN GO launched in 2024.
- **Loop:** Buy NFT sneakers (Solana / BNB / ETH). Walk or run outdoors to earn GST (unlimited supply utility token). Spend GST on repairs, levelling and minting new sneakers. GMT is the capped governance token. Energy caps limit earning sessions per day.
- **Peak:** About $4.19 GMT and $9.36 GST (April 2022). More than 1M DAU at peak. Entry sneakers cost over $1,000 (May 2022).
- **Collapse:**
  - GST fell 98% in 2 months.
  - The May 2022 exit from mainland China (GMT -37% in minutes).
  - By October sneakers sold for about $51.
  - Layoffs (Decrypt).
- **Why it failed:** It was a **Ponzi-shaped dual-token economy**. Rewards to existing users were paid by new entrants buying sneakers. Once user growth stalled, the faucet (GST emission) outpaced sinks, the price collapsed, and break-even stretched to about 144 days. Users came for yield, not fitness, so they left when the yield did. Regulatory shock (China) and bot or multi-account farming added to it.
- **Store:** STEPN iOS 4.23 (3,024). GP `com.bcy.fsapp` 3.94, 1M+. STEPN GO iOS 4.71 (136), GP 4.4 (2,080), 50k+.
- **Lesson:** **Never make extrinsic financial yield the core loop.** Rewards must be funded from outside the player base (advertisers, employers, insurers) or be non-monetary.

### 17. Other step-reward apps: WeWard, CashWalk, Stepler, StepSetGo, Winwalk
- **WeWard** (France): GP 4.17 (160,594), 10M+, about 16.7M installs. Claims 20M users and $20M+ paid out (company). PayPal or Venmo cash, slow.
- **CashWalk** (Korea, CASHWALK LABS): iOS 4.57 (61,337). Korean GP version 3.91 (207,881), 10M+. Gift cards only, coins collected by hand, lock-screen ads.
- **Stepler** (Stepler AB, Sweden): iOS 4.38 (98), claims 10M users. Points and "diamonds" for products, plus a paid Coach subscription.
- **StepSetGo** (India): GP 10M+.
- **Winwalk:** GP 5M+.
- **Lesson:** Commodity apps funded by lock-screen ads, offer walls and affiliate deals. Large install counts and low ratings. Users see them as "side hustles", not health tools.

### 18. Charity Miles
- **Developer:** Charity Miles LLC (Gene Gurkoff). iOS 2012.
- **Loop:** Each mile walked, run or cycled earns sponsor money for a charity you pick (historically $0.25/mile running, $0.10/mile cycling). Team pledges. Corporate challenges (e.g. Bank of America employees raised $103k).
- **Store:** iOS **4.78 (65,319)**. GP **2.17** (7,751), 1M+ band.
- **Scale:** More than $2.5M raised for charity (company, circa 2017). The company raised $1.7M in venture funding (CB Insights).
- **Complaints (GP):** GPS and tracking bugs, sponsor money dried up, an ad-heavy/pledge-ask model.
- **Lesson:** Altruism is a strong motivator, as the iOS rating shows. Sponsor-funded pools are fragile, and the Android experience fell apart.

## C. Platform and wearable gamification: rings, badges, segments, leaderboards

### 19. Strava (challenges, segments, KOM/QOM, Local Legends)
- **Developer:** Strava Inc. (SF). Launched 2009, iOS 2011.
- **Mechanics:**
  - **Segments** are user-defined stretches of road or trail with all-time leaderboards. KOM/QOM crowns go to the fastest.
  - **Local Legend** (2020) goes to whoever completes a segment most often in 90 days. It rewards consistency over speed.
  - Monthly challenges with digital badges and sponsored challenges (brands pay).
  - Kudos as social likes. Clubs. Streaks. Year in Sport recap (Wrapped-style).
- **Monetization:** Freemium. Premium $79.99/yr or $11.99/mo. Sponsored challenges.
- **Store:** iOS 4.81 (381,889). GP 4.61 (1.22M), 100M+, about 139M installs. Runna (acquired 2025): iOS 4.85 (35,997).
- **Business:** Valued at $2.2B (May 2025, Sequoia-led), about $500M ARR run-rate, 150M+ registered users, about 50M MAU (Sensor Tower). Acquired Runna and The Breakaway in 2025. IPO filing reported Jan 2026 *(status conflicting)*.
- **Problems:** Cheating (e-bikes, cars, motorbikes setting KOMs). In March 2026 Strava **removed 2.3M e-bike activities** from leaderboards using ML and gave top-10 places back to 293k athletes. Privacy (2018 heatmap revealed military bases). Paywalling of segment leaderboards (2020) annoyed users. KOM chasing has been linked to risky riding.
- **Lessons:** A **social graph plus effort-based status (kudos)** beats points. Competitive leaderboards need constant anti-cheat work. Local Legend shows a consistency-based honour can sit next to an elite one. Strava is "Instagram for exercise", and the gamification serves social sharing.

### 20. Apple Fitness (Activity rings, awards, competitions)
- **Developer:** Apple. Rings arrived with Apple Watch in 2015. The Fitness app (iOS) received an "Apple Fitness" store page in 2017. Fitness+ launched in 2020 ($9.99/mo or $79.99/yr).
- **Mechanics:**
  - Three rings (Move, Exercise, Stand). Close all three every day.
  - Streaks, "Perfect Week" and "Perfect Month" awards. Limited-edition awards (Heart Month, Earth Day, International Women's Day, the **January "Ring in the New Year" 7-day streak**), each with an animated 3D medal.
  - 7-day head-to-head Activity Competitions with friends (points up to 600/day). Shared activity.
  - watchOS 11 (2024) added pause days and adjustable goals per weekday, after long-standing complaints about streak anxiety.
- **Store:** The Apple Fitness app page iOS 2.86 (11,706). Ratings mostly reflect Fitness+ and the iPhone-only mode.
- **Efficacy:** Apple's Heart and Movement Study data (about 100k participants, 4 years) shows January exercise increases are sustained, linked to the January award (Apple press release Jan 2026, not peer-reviewed).
- **Users love:** Very simple. Rings are a "visible to-do" on the wrist. Competitions with friends are addictive.
- **Complaints:** Streak anxiety. People walking circles at 11pm. Sick days broke streaks (until 2024). Move ring "punishes" rest.
- **Lesson:** **Three simple daily targets shown as a closable shape is the most copied fitness game mechanic.** Apple's own retreat into rest days shows that strict streaks eventually hurt users.

### 21. Fitbit (badges, challenges) / Google Health app
- **Developer:** Fitbit, acquired by Google in 2021. The iOS app is now titled "Google Health (Fitbit)".
- **Mechanics:** Lifetime badges for steps, floors and distance (e.g. "Penguin March", "Africa"). Daily badges. Friends' weekly leaderboard. **Challenges** (Workweek Hustle, Weekend Warrior) and **Adventures** (virtual walks in Yosemite and elsewhere) were **removed on 27 Mar 2023** along with open groups.
- **Store:** iOS 4.46 (713,968). GP **3.6** (1.33M), 100M+, about 317M installs.
- **Backlash:** Large community protest threads (50+ pages), Tom's Guide "huge mistake", users switching to Garmin.
- **Lesson:** **Social challenges were the core of retention, and removing them caused heavy reputational damage.** This is an important cautionary tale: users take any gamified social feature you remove as a personal loss.

### 22. Garmin Connect (badges, challenges)
- **Developer:** Garmin. iOS 2013.
- **Mechanics:** Hundreds of badges with point values that build a "level" (Level 1–10). Connect challenges (Weekend Warrior, step challenges), group challenges, Badge Database fan sites. Body Battery and Training Readiness scores. Free (the Connect+ subscription launched in 2025 *(unverified details)*).
- **Store:** iOS 4.40 (60,068). GP 4.4 (1.12M), 50M+, about 52M installs.
- **Lesson:** A hardcore badge-hunting community exists. Garmin gained ex-Fitbit users after the 2023 removals.

### 23. Nike Run Club
- **Developer:** Nike. iOS 2010 (as Nike+).
- **Mechanics:** Audio-guided runs (with coaches and athletes), achievements and trophies (fastest 5k, streaks, monthly challenges), run-level colours (yellow → orange → green → blue → purple → black → volt), leaderboards with friends, Cheers.
- **Monetization:** Free, a marketing channel for Nike's sales ecosystem.
- **Store:** iOS **4.77 (420,730)**. GP 4.24 (1.11M), 50M+, about 58M installs.
- **Complaints:** Features removed (shoe tracking, web access), sync issues, the app went stagnant during Nike's cuts.
- **Lesson:** Level colours are simple status markers. Free plus guided audio builds loyalty.

### 24. Peloton (badges, streaks, leaderboards, Club Peloton)
- **Developer:** Peloton Interactive. App 2014 (iOS).
- **Mechanics:** Live leaderboard during classes (output in kJ), instructor shout-outs for milestones (e.g. a "century ride"), daily and weekly streak badges, challenge badges, a trophy cabinet. **Club Peloton** (2025): a loyalty tier from Bronze to Legend. Points come from **consistency, not output**: 2 points per workout day, streak-length points and a team point each week. Rewards are apparel discounts of up to 25%.
- **Monetization:** All-Access $44/mo with hardware. App One/Plus tiers about $12.99 to $24 per month.
- **Store:** iOS **4.92 (819,709)**. GP 4.01 (15,399), 1M+, about 3.8M installs.
- **Lesson:** Live leaderboards create intense moments. The 2025 redesign moved rewards toward consistency, which is in line with the industry shift away from rewarding performance.

### 25. WHOOP (strain / recovery as a game)
- **Developer:** Whoop Inc. (Boston). App 2015.
- **Mechanics:** Daily **Strain** score (0–21) vs **Recovery** % (green/yellow/red). Sleep Performance. Journal behaviour tracking. Teams and leaderboards. "Healthspan" / WHOOP Age. The "game" is balancing strain against recovery, and recovery colours act as a daily verdict.
- **Monetization:** Hardware included with membership ($199–$359/yr tiers, 2025).
- **Store:** iOS 4.81 (97,331). GP 4.75 (32,823), 1M+.
- **Business:** $10.1B valuation (Series G, $575M, March 2026). About $1.1B annualized bookings in 2025, 2.5M+ members (Sacra estimates).
- **Lesson:** A single daily number with a colour verdict creates a **morning check-in habit**, which is gamification through feedback rather than rewards.

### 26. Oura
- **Developer:** Oura Health Oy (Finland). App 2016.
- **Mechanics:** Readiness, Sleep and Activity scores (0–100). Crowns for scores of 85+. Activity goal tracking. Badges and trends. Circles social sharing (2024–25).
- **Monetization:** Ring ($299+) plus $5.99/mo membership.
- **Store:** iOS 4.86 (327,329). GP 4.72 (43,380), 1M+.
- **Business:** **About $11B valuation** (Oct 2025, $900M Series E). More than 5.5M rings sold (about 3M of them in 2025). About $1B revenue in 2025 (about 80% hardware, 20% subscription). Filed confidentially for IPO.
- **Lesson:** Scores and crowns are a light gamification layer on top of real biomarkers. Mass-market users like the "crown" moments.

### 27. Gentler Streak
- **Developer:** Gentler Stories d.o.o. (Slovenia, team of about 8). iOS Sep 2021. iOS / watchOS only.
- **Mechanics:** An **"Activity Path"**: a 10-day zone that adapts to your fitness and recovery and tells you to push, maintain or rest. The streak counts days "within your path", so rest days count. Yorhart is a mascot character. Monthly summaries.
- **Monetization:** Freemium. Premium about $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr *(unverified)*.
- **Store:** iOS 4.71 (8,897).
- **Awards:** Apple Watch App of the Year 2022. **Apple Design Award 2024 (Social Impact)**.
- **Lesson:** "Compassionate gamification", where the streak rewards appropriate behaviour rather than maximum behaviour, is a recognised and award-winning design position. It is a direct counter to Apple's ring-streak anxiety.

### 28. Bevel
- **Developer:** Starlight Tech LLC (NYC). iOS Jan 2024.
- **Mechanics:** Recovery, Strain and Sleep scores from Apple Watch data, plus an energy bank, nutrition logging, glucose integration and an AI coach. A "Whoop without the strap".
- **Business:** $10M Series A from General Catalyst (Oct 2025). Moved from paywall to almost entirely free (Dec 2025).
- **Store:** iOS **4.85 (18,151)**.
- **Lesson:** Software-only clones of Whoop-style scores on Apple Watch data are growing fast.

### 38. Noom
- **Developer:** Noom Inc. (NYC). App 2011 (Android first).

## Patterns, gaps and lessons

### 1. What the evidence says

- **Gamification works, modestly and mostly short-term.**
  - The Mazeas et al. 2022 JMIR meta-analysis (16 studies, n=2,407) found g = 0.42, about **+1,610 steps/day**, with a wide CI (372 to 2,847).
  - Pokémon GO gave about +955 steps/day, **gone by week 6**.
  - Sweatcoin users gained +18.7–19.5% steps at 6 months, with no effect at 12 months in an independent study.
- **Competition sticks best.** In the STEP UP trial (Patel, JAMA IM 2019, n=602), all three arms (support, collaboration, competition) raised steps. **Only competition stayed above control after the game ended** (+569 steps/day at follow-up; about +920 during the intervention).
- **Money incentives work while they last.** Deposit contracts and wagers (Volpp 2008; 2016 JAMA IM: 39% lost at least 5%) produce real short-term weight loss and regain is common. HealthyWage self-reports 30–40% winners. DietBet and StepBet games anecdotally have 60–70% winners.
- **Content-rich behaviour-change modules beat bare counters.** Smoke Free's daily missions nearly doubled 3-month abstinence (exploratory RCT, n=28k).
- **Sleep gamification has weak but positive signals.** For Pokémon Sleep, 45% of users increased sleep time (Tsukuba 2025), and the study is not randomised.

### 2. Mechanics that keep working (ranked by how often they show up in winners)

1. **Social accountability and belonging:** Ladder teams, Strava kudos/clubs, Peloton live leaderboard, I Am Sober community, Fitbit challenges (proven by the backlash when they were removed).
2. **A single daily verdict** (rings, Whoop recovery colour, Oura crown, Rise sleep debt, Gentler Path). This creates a morning or evening check-in ritual.
3. **Collection with a cozy fiction** (Pikmin Bloom, Pokémon Sleep, Plant Nanny, Waterllama, Finch, Walkr). This has the highest ratings and slow-burn revenue growth. Pikmin's revenue grew every year.
4. **Story that pulls you out the door** (Zombies, Run!). Hard to copy and expensive to keep up.
5. **Finite challenges with a visible grid** (30-day, 75 Hard, Apple monthly and limited-edition awards). Easy to commit to because there is an end, and they create a shareable identity.
6. **Territory and place** (Pokémon GO, INTVL, Run An Empire, KYRO). The breakout mechanic of 2024–26, spread by TikTok.
7. **Anime "level up / System" framing** (Arise and its clones). A powerful acquisition hook in 2025–26 with unknown retention.

### 3. What fails or backfires

- **Financialised core loops collapse.** STEPN (GST -98% in 2 months, sneakers $1,000 → $51) and Sweatcoin's SWEAT token (ATL $0.000175, wallet ends 30 Dec 2026). Users who come for yield leave when the yield goes. Rewards have to be paid from outside the player base.
- **Micro-cash rewards turn into an ad-farm.** CashWalk, WeWard and step-to-earn apps get many installs and are seen as side hustles, with weak health identity.
- **Strict streaks cause anxiety and drop-off.** Apple added pause days in 2024. 75 Hard's reset-to-day-1 led to "75 Soft". Gentler Streak won an Apple Design Award by rewarding rest. Club Peloton moved rewards to consistency.
- **Performance leaderboards invite cheating and exclude beginners.** Strava purged 2.3M e-bike activities in 2026. Strava added Local Legend (frequency rather than speed) to give normal people a crown.
- **Removing loved social features loses trust** (Fitbit 2023).
- **Platform and parent-company risk:** Supernatural (Meta, maintenance mode 2026), Zombies, Run! (OliveX era layoffs), Fitbit under Google.
- **Wagering business models profit from failure.** This creates ethical tension and invites weigh-in gaming and crash dieting.
- **Dark-pattern billing** (Noom's $62M settlement, 75 Hard's switch to subscription, Rise having no monthly plan) shows up widely in reviews.
- **Hardware-dependent games** (VR, Ring Fit) sell big in bursts and then fade. Most people do not keep the hardware out.

### 4. Gaps and opportunities for a self-improvement gamification app

1. **One avatar or world for all health domains.** Today a person needs Pikmin (walking), Waterllama (water), Pokémon Sleep (sleep), I Am Sober (quitting), Ladder (strength) and Finch (self-care). **No single game ties movement, sleep, hydration, nutrition and quitting into one progression with health-kit integration and a cozy or RPG world.** Finch comes closest and is light on physical health. Habitica is manual and dated.
2. **Rewards for consistency, compassion and adaptation, inside a game.** Gentler Streak's adaptive path is well liked and has no game layer. Arise-style RPGs have a game and punish rest. The space for an "RPG that rewards recovery" is open.
3. **Social groups at Ladder/Strava quality for non-athletes.** Small parties or guilds with co-op goals (STEP UP evidence favours competition, so allow friendly team-vs-team), without performance leaderboards that intimidate beginners.
4. **Money on the line without a casino feel.** Charity stakes (lose → money goes to charity), employer- or insurer-funded pools, or "pledge" mechanics (I Am Sober style) can capture loss aversion without the house profiting from failure.
5. **Auto-tracked passive inputs feeding a game.** HealthKit/Health Connect steps, sleep and workouts should power the game automatically, the way Pikmin Bloom and Pokémon Sleep do. Manual logging (Habitica) churns.
6. **Narrative content at sustainable cost.** Zombies, Run! shows stories retain users. AI-assisted or modular narrative could provide the content treadmill that killed Supernatural and slowed Zombies, Run!.
7. **Finite "seasons" and challenges as the default structure** (30-day / 75-day / monthly awards) with shareable identity cards for TikTok, the channel that grew INTVL and 75 Hard.
8. **Anti-cheat and verification** must be designed in if there are leaderboards or stakes (StepBet sync failures, DietBet weigh-in hacks, Strava e-bikes).

### 5. Design rules distilled

- Let the game pull the behaviour (fiction, collection, place). Do not pay people to do it.
- One clear daily verdict plus a weekly arc plus finite seasons.
- Count rest and partial days (forgiving streaks, "streak freezes", adaptive targets).
- Make social the core retention layer, with cooperation by default and opt-in competition.
- If money is involved, external funding or charity, never a token economy.
- Track passively wherever possible, with reflection or ritual (pledge and review) on top.
- Do not remove social features, and avoid dark-pattern billing. Both cost reviews that rarely recover.
